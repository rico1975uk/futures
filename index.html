Take it further

Dig deeper with your own AI assistant

Our report only knows what's in the published data and your answers. An AI assistant that can search the web, such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT, can fill the gaps: admissions distances, transition programmes, behaviour policies, clubs and open-evening dates. Copy a prompt, paste it into your assistant, and check the links it gives you.

What to include in the prompts

Whatever you paste goes to that AI company under your own account and its privacy terms. The prompts never include your full address or your individual answers.
